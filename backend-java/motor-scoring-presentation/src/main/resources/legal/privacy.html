Motor Scoring Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

This policy describes the data handling of the Motor Scoring development and sandbox environment.

1. Information processed

Depending on how you use Motor Scoring, the service may process account information such as email address, authentication identifiers, role and permission information, email-verification status, multi-factor-authentication configuration, session information, and data submitted for the credit-scoring workflow.

2. Social login data

When you sign in with a supported identity provider, Motor Scoring receives only the information authorized for that login flow. For TikTok Login Kit with the basic profile scope, this may include the provider user identifier and basic profile information made available by TikTok. Provider access tokens are used to complete the login flow and are not intended to be retained as long-term application credentials for login-only use.

3. Why information is used

Information is processed to create and secure accounts, verify identity, complete authentication and MFA, manage sessions and authorization, operate the scoring workflow, provide requested application functionality, diagnose technical problems, and protect the service against misuse.

4. Security

Motor Scoring uses security controls designed for the project architecture, including encrypted transport, password hashing, signed application access tokens, protected refresh sessions, and encrypted storage of MFA secrets. No security mechanism can guarantee absolute protection.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be exchanged with service providers only as needed to deliver configured functionality, such as Google or TikTok for social authentication and an email provider for verification or notifications. The project does not require selling personal information as part of its described functionality.

6. Retention

Account, session, authentication, and scoring records may be retained for as long as needed to operate and test the environment, satisfy security requirements, or support the project workflow. Because this is a development environment, test data may also be reset or deleted during maintenance.

7. Your choices

You may stop using social login and stop using the service. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of data in this sandbox should be directed to the administrator or owner who provided access to the environment.

8. Third-party policies

Third-party identity providers process information under their own privacy policies. Motor Scoring does not control the independent privacy practices of those providers.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated as the project and its integrations change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the administrator or owner who provided access to this Motor Scoring environment.
